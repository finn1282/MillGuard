# General rules for every fault note

Written by the MillGuard team in our own words. Generic maintenance practice, not a manufacturer procedure.

- Any job that opens or touches a machine needs lock-out/tag-out first: switch off, isolate energy (electrical, hydraulic pressure, steam), lock, tag, and test that it cannot move.
- Compare against the machine's own normal, not a textbook number. MillGuard quotes changes against the machine's own previous 30 days.
- Record what was found and what was replaced in the work order. The next alert for the same machine learns from it.
- Exact limits, clearances, torques, part numbers and oil grades come from the manufacturer's manual and the mill's own SOP. We do not supply them.
