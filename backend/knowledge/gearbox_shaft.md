# Gearbox or shaft fault

- Worn gear teeth, shaft misalignment or tired gear oil raise friction, so gearbox oil temperature, vibration and motor current per tonne drift up together.
- Practical order: check oil level and take a sample, look for metal particles on the magnetic plug, at the stop (locked out) check shaft alignment and coupling bolts, change oil and filter, look at the teeth through the inspection port, and replace the gear set or shaft only if damage is seen. Log what was found.
- Intermediate gears appear among the higher-risk parts in screw-press FMEA case studies.
- No external source is cited for the steps above: they are general good practice from our playbook, not a manufacturer procedure.
