# Boiler steam temperature drift

- Steam temperature should sit in a steady band for a given load. A sustained drop or swing can come from feed-water dosing or blowdown that is off, changes in combustion or fuel quality, or fouled heat-transfer surfaces.
- Scale or deposits insulate boiler surfaces, which hurts heat transfer and can overheat tube metal. This is why steam temperature and feed-water quality are watched together.
- Practical order: check the feed-water dosing and the blowdown settings, compare steam temperature and pressure with the load, look at fuel feed and combustion, and plan any deeper inspection for a boiler stop. A boiler stop stops the whole mill, so schedule it deliberately.
- Safety: steam, hot surfaces and stored pressure. Follow the mill's boiler SOP and the competent person's rules.
