# Cone hydraulic fault

- The cone is held by a hydraulic system. If the pressure swings, rises or spikes, the squeeze on the fruit changes and the press behaves unevenly.
- Common causes in hydraulic systems generally: internal leakage past worn seals (often no visible drip), air in the oil from a suction-side leak or low tank level, a worn or dirty relief or compensator valve that "hunts", a tired pump, and contamination with water or particles.
- Sensible diagnostic order: oil level and filters first, then air and the suction side, then external leaks on hoses and fittings, then internal leakage and the relief valve.
- Practical fix order for this mill: check oil level and look for leaks, watch the gauge for swings while running, at the stop (locked out and pressure released) replace leaking hoses and worn seals, change the hydraulic filter, top up with the oil grade the manufacturer names, and test the cone pressure under load before handing back.
- Always release stored hydraulic pressure before opening any line.
