# Boiler feed-water quality drift

- Conductivity is the usual quick measure of dissolved solids in boiler water. When it climbs, scale and carry-over become more likely; scale insulates the tubes, tube metal overheats, and tubes can fail. Blowdown and feed-water treatment are the two levers.
- Vendor sources quote different alarm numbers and units, so use the boiler manufacturer's and the mill's own limits, not a number from us.
- Practical order: test feed-water conductivity and hardness, check the water-treatment dosing and softener, check the blowdown schedule and valves, then plan an inspection of the tubes for scale at the next stop.
- A tube leak stops the whole mill, which is why this fault is ranked first when it appears.
