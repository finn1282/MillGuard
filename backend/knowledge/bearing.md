# Bearing wear

- A rolling bearing that is starting to fail makes small impacts. Vibration "spikiness" (kurtosis) rises early, before overall vibration level does; overall level and housing temperature rise later.
- Kurtosis is cheap and good for early warning, but noisy: it should be read as a trend over days, together with vibration level and temperature, not as a single reading.
- Common causes: too little or dirty lubricant, contamination, misalignment, overloading.
- Practical order: check grease or oil level and condition, listen and feel for roughness and heat while running, and at the stop (locked out) open the housing, replace the bearing and seals, clean and re-grease, restart and compare vibration with the earlier reading.
- Thrust and roller bearings appear among the highest-risk parts in screw-press FMEA case studies, mainly through poor lubrication and overload.
