Fault guide

Worm wear

Gradual — predictable

What it is

The screw flights that squeeze the fruit wear down.

Fingerprint

Current per tonne up, oil in fibre up, throughput steady.

Why it matters

More oil lost, then the press stops.

Standard fix

Replace the worm set at a planned stop.

Bearing damage

Gradual — predictable

What it is

A rolling bearing starts to crack or pit.

Fingerprint

Vibration spikes first, then overall vibration rises.

Why it matters

A seized bearing can bend the shaft.

Standard fix

Replace the bearing; check lubrication.

Gearbox / shaft

Gradual — predictable

What it is

Gears or the drive shaft wear or misalign.

Fingerprint

Gearbox oil temperature drifts up.

Why it matters

Gearbox repairs take days.

Standard fix

Change oil, inspect gears and shaft.

Hydraulic cone

Gradual — predictable

What it is

The cone loses pressing pressure.

Fingerprint

Cone pressure unstable or dropping.

Why it matters

More oil left in the fibre.

Standard fix

Check hydraulic oil, seals, accumulator.

Electrical

Sudden — hard to predict

What it is

Motor, starter or cable fault.

Fingerprint

Current spikes or drops out, little warning.

Why it matters

The press stops at once.

Standard fix

Check motor, starter and cables.

Boiler steam

Gradual — predictable

What it is

Steam temperature leaves its safe band.

Fingerprint

Outside 530–545 °C, swinging.

Why it matters

Low steam stops the whole mill.

Standard fix

Check fuel feed and combustion air.

Boiler water

Gradual — predictable

What it is

Dissolved solids build up in the water.

Fingerprint

Conductivity up; blowdown overdue.

Why it matters

Scale and tube damage — whole-mill stop.

Standard fix

Check dosing, blow down, test the water.
